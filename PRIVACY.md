# Privacy policy

The Celestellaris Installer does not collect, store or transmit any personal data, and has no analytics or telemetry.

What it does on your computer:
- Looks for your Celeste install folder (Steam, Epic, GOG, itch app) on your own disk.
- Downloads Everest from the official Everest GitHub release and helper mods from the public Everest mod
  database. These servers (GitHub and the mod mirror) may log your IP address as with any download. This
  project does not receive that information.
- Writes files only into your Celeste folder and its `Mods` folder.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/Magedeline/celestellaris-installer/issues
