# Notices and what you can rebrand

## Included / used
- **Everest** (https://github.com/EverestAPI/Everest) is MIT licensed. The installer downloads it from
  the official release. If you ever fork or bundle it, keep its LICENSE and copyright notice and
  say what you changed.
- **Helper mods** are downloaded from the public Everest mod database. They keep their own authors and licences.
- **Celeste** is Maddy Makes Games' game. Players must own it. Never ship Celeste.exe, Content, sprites
  or music in your download.

## What you can freely rebrand
- The installer's name, window, icon and colours (`branding.json`).
- Your own mod's content, which is the sprites, dialog, maps and UI you made yourself in Celestellaris.
- A fork of Everest under a new name, if you keep the MIT notice. Replacing Celeste's own sprites with your own
  art should go in your mod's `Graphics` folder (Everest's Atlas override), not by editing Celeste's files.

## What you can't
- Redistribute Celeste's original art, audio or code, even renamed.
- Remove Everest's licence text or imply it was written by you.
